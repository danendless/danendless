# Hi, I'm Daniel Endless

**`Data Scientist`**

I'm a bilingual (English–Portuguese) professional with a multidisciplinary background in data science, computer science, technical communication, and business operations. I combine analytical thinking, technical problem-solving, and clear communication with a systems-oriented approach to developing practical, reliable, and well-documented solutions.

I hold a B.A. in English from the University of Massachusetts Boston, with a focus on technical and professional communication, and have also pursued undergraduate studies in computer science. I'm a TripleTen Data Science Bootcamp graduate, with hands-on project experience in Python, SQL, statistics, machine learning, and predictive modeling.

My professional experience spans technical writing and localization, data analysis, reporting automation, business process improvement, and enterprise system implementations, including Oracle NetSuite ERP and Coupa Procurement in the biotechnology industry.

Driven by curiosity and continuous learning, I enjoy understanding how systems work, exploring complex problems, and applying technology and data to solve real-world challenges.

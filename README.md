# Hi, I'm Daniel Endless

**`Data Scientist`**

I'm a bilingual (English–Portuguese) professional with a multidisciplinary background in data science, computer science, technical communication, and business operations.

I hold a B.A. in English from the University of Massachusetts Boston, with a focus on technical and professional communication, and have a background in computer science. I am also a TripleTen Data Science Bootcamp graduate, having earned my certificate after completing intensive training and practical projects in Python, SQL, statistical analysis, machine learning, and predictive modeling.

My professional experience spans technical writing and localization, business process optimization, data analysis, reporting automation, and multiple enterprise systems implementation, including work in the biotechnology industry.

I combine analytical thinking, technical proficiency, problem-solving, and strong communication skills with a multidisciplinary perspective. Driven by curiosity and continuous learning, I enjoy exploring complex problems, understanding how systems work, and developing practical, well-documented solutions.

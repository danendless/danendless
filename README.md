# Hi, I'm Daniel Endless

**`Data Scientist`**

I'm a bilingual (English–Portuguese) professional with a multidisciplinary background spanning data science, computer science, technical communication, and business operations.

I hold a B.A. in English from the University of Massachusetts Boston, with a focus on technical and professional communication, and have also pursued undergraduate studies in computer science. I'm a TripleTen Data Science Bootcamp graduate, with hands-on project experience in Python, SQL, statistics, machine learning, and predictive modeling.

My professional experience includes technical writing and localization, data analysis, reporting automation, business process improvement, and enterprise system implementations, including Oracle NetSuite ERP and Coupa Procurement in the biotechnology industry.

I bring together analytical thinking, technical problem-solving, and clear communication with a systems-oriented perspective. Driven by curiosity and continuous learning, I enjoy understanding how things work, exploring complex problems, and developing practical, reliable, and well-documented solutions.
